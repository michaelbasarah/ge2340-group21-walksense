## 23 Nov 2026: two-floor session, 60 s stops, gyroscope

**Added**

## 24 Nov 2026: two sessions (sesi 1: four phones, sesi 2: two phones)

**Added**
- sesi 1: 220 unique recordings (27 stops + 28 walks), phones a55, a56, iphone, s24 [23]
- sesi 2: 108 recordings (26 stops + 28 walks), phones iphone, s24 [23]
- first independent second pass: train one session, test the other (no same-day leakage) [23, 30]
- accelerometer logged at stops, so tilt features are possible at stops [30]
- same-handset cross-day (ip16, 23 Nov to 24 s2): 13/26 = 50.0%, reverse 18/42 = 42.9% [30]
- weighted fused kNN, honest: 122/295 = 41.4% leave one session out, 106/295 = 35.9% by stop; leaky random windows 1349/1363 = 99.0% [32]

**Failures and problems**
- the 'iphone' slot was two different handsets (iPhone19,2 in s1, iPhone17,4 in s2) [30, 37]
- S24 barometer unusable for floors: largest same-floor consecutive change 5.08 hPa in s1 [23, 30]
- floor step tracking broke after a long break in s1: 23/27 = 85.2%; largest same-floor change 0.833 hPa [30]
- Wi-Fi still not a fingerprint: each BSSID about once per stop (1.08 rows per BSSID in s2) [31]
- pooling all phones in one kNN worse than per-phone model [32]
- very strong field (above 100 uT) at the 4F escalators in s2 on both phones, cause unknown [34]

**Lessons / what changed**
- most of the |B| spread is handset and day, not place: place share of |B| variance 14.5%, phones 47.0%, sessions 34.4% [38]
- phone held the same way (flat, facing the door) makes raw x/y/z work; tilt adds nothing on top [24, 30]
- 20 s pieces triple n but not the information; removing pieces raised accuracy by removing hard test pieces: LOSO 375/842 = 44.5% on pieces [40]

Log entries: 23, 30 to 38, 40. Data: phase2/clean-data/2026-11-24/
